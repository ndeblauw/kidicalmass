## 1. Governance & structure — why KM works

- KM survives because of **continuity and structure**, not in spite of it. *"Als iemand anders dat gedaan had, dan was de bal ineengestort... omdat ik continuïteit en structuur bied, is het kunnen blijven bestaan."* If it had been run "anarchistically" it would have collapsed after last year.
- Structure = the things that hold durable engagement: regular inspraakmomenten, support, a shared communication format, one tone of voice, one message. *"De tone-of-voice, de message, dat een beetje keiharder is, dat je niet overal iets anders gaat doen."*
- Quality is held by **guidelines you hand down, not enforce**. There is a written **huishoudelijk reglement / charter ("ROI")**. *"guidelines die je meegeeft en niet afdwingt."* A few people dislike the firmness; most are fine with it.
- Positioning nuance: not pure "kids vs cars", also for the *potential* cyclists; slightly more activist — **but the recommendations must stay light, or the mass won't come**. *"Het moeten light aanbevelingen zijn, want anders gaat het de grote massa niet aanspreken. Als het niche en hardcore is — fiets, fiets, fiets — dan gaat het niet werken."*
- Light meeting culture on purpose: calls them **"meetups", not "assemblée générale"**, max ~30 min, then a drink. Does not want a vergadercultuur. Contrast with Fietsersbond's 3-hour meetings.

## 2. Scale & operating reality

- Coordination team + **4 meetups/year** + ~**15–20 local groups**.
- One big "trekkers" WhatsApp (~30 people) + ~20 local groups (~15 people each) → **~300 active people**, ~30 in the general group. New groups starting: Mechelen, Leuven. Platform fragmentation: Mons on Telegram, Laken on Signal.
- **Deliberately NOT a cross-community platform.** Wants strong *local* communities; if a group is short-staffed it should recruit locally, not borrow from the neighbouring group. *"Ik wil echt lokale communities creëren... anders gaan ze uit gemak zeggen: we laten die van die groep komen."*
- North Brussels is hard (poorer, harder/less safe cycling, traffic aggression); South Brussels has ~30 volunteers/commune and largely self-runs. Some groups go dormant and need re-activation via local café meetups / "ronde van de gemeente".

## 3. Funding — the biggest unstated objective

- **Funding is one of the biggest organisational goals and was not in the analysis.** *"Wij hebben eigenlijk geld nodig... ik wil fulltime kunnen aanwerven. Dat is één van de grootste doelstellingen, dat er centen binnenkomen."*
- Frederik noted live: if that's the most important thing, it belongs on essentially every page.
- **Membership = "spacefunding"** via the Growthfunding platform: recurring (a subscription), €36/yr ≈ €3/mo, includes a t-shirt, makes you a co-financer. Important for government legitimacy and self-sufficiency ("we willen niet moeten beheren van: heb jij al gestort?").
- Currently on the homepage but **not linked to "Membership"**; not boosted; payment module is poor (drop-off after a few tries).
- Prefer **individual donations over grant dossiers**: *"we willen niet meer dossiers, we willen vooral individuele donaties."* Public funding ok; private/"from anyone" story not wanted.
- The website should **reflect the future structure/governance**: *"De website geeft de structuur aan van de toekomstige werking."*

## 4. Audiences — confirmations & corrections

**First-time family:** confirmed — "is this for us? can we join? where/when next? practical." "No bike" is a very small % (like Fietsbieb). Maybe also wants to engage, but probably not on the first visit.

**Regular family (no Facebook):** confirmed wanting it on the site. Nuance: *"bijna niemand zit nog op Facebook"* — **but Leticia actively uses Facebook event "interested" counts to gauge turnout and decide whether to mobilise more.** Web page visits are "less binding" than an FB "interested" click. So FB events still do real operational work.

**Pink vest / volunteer:** recruited at the end of a ride (phone number → WhatsApp). Onboarding is weak: new people often arrive clueless ("ik weet van niks"), having missed the local meetup. Hard to see which groups need help — wants a way to keep history/signals.

**Local organiser / chapter lead:** wants own page to send people to (Violet, an IT-er, asked for it). Wants a **fixed structure they "can't mess up"**. Data is entered manually (agenda hand-typed). Partners differ per chapter (many fields). Own email per chapter + a general one.

**Scaling — important correction:** *"Ik weet niet of de uitdaging meer groepen is, want we hebben er echt al veel. Het is meer om meer deelnemers te hebben dan meer groepen."* ~45 groups already, 6 new coming. New groups without good support get demotivated and die. **The challenge is more participants in existing groups, not maximising new groups.**

**Local > mass:** local rides are the real product — nicer, with neighbours, "we want change *here*"; more relaxing than 1000–2000-person one-shot mass rides abroad.

## 5. Events & the agenda workflow (as-is)

- ~**50 events/season**. Agenda is **hand-typed**. Chapters propose dates → Leticia coordinates: **max 2 kiddicals per weekend**, spread out, avoid school holidays, first-come-first-served slot picking via a shared **Excel**.
- Chapters then plan the route in WhatsApp (proposal → refined collectively → final). Leticia takes the final version into comms: **InDesign posters/flyers**, prints, a logistic "vlinder" (Cyclo courier) distributes printed material to groups.
- Leticia publishes the pages, posts in WhatsApp; groups share. Also posts FB events, VisitBrussels (a duplicate).
- **Per-ride pages → really per-gemeente:** one page per gemeente listing all that gemeente's rides; past rides drop off; events auto-populate. Individual event pages still valued for: shareable link, no-Facebook users, "I'm attending"/hosts.
- Making own FB events = more work; only worth it if it doesn't add work.
- Chapters can fill their own ride data once the Excel-equivalent structure is solid; can choose their own photo.

## 6. Subscriptions / newsletter

- Subscribe to a chapter → get mail when new rides appear. **Frequency is low: rides are created ~once or twice a year** (Jan & Sept, school-year rhythm) — not a rolling content stream. No studiedienst for regular content.
- 500-address mailing list barely used because content isn't localised/adapted. Wants per-chapter/region: *"de volgende in de buurt"* + latest press/photos. Nearby gemeentes also relevant (people attend neighbouring rides).

## 7. Site scope — what Leticia confirmed in / out

**OUT / not needed:**
- **Webshop** — not visible, not wanted as a shop. The t-shirt is the spacefunding membership; people buy it there. *"de webshop doen we niet."*
- **Photo gallery system** — not needed. A few photos per chapter to show it's fun; not per-ride galleries (rides go to social media). Confirms the cut.
- **Poster/flyer auto-generation** — explored and dropped: a mini-editor is too much to build; she wants InDesign-quality layout and has graphic interns. *"dan laten we het vallen, het is niet het voornaamste."*
- **Worked-out "start a chapter" intake flow** — not now; enough groups, don't over-invest (see §4).

**IN / wanted:**
- **Membership / spacefunding** prominent (see §3) — needs a clear page + linking the t-shirt to Membership.
- **"Zo word je vrijwilliger"** page — exists but unclear; wants clear steps, more worked out. Volunteer form **routed to the local responsible** (not the central mailbox); a local email link; the person also gets a reminder mail explaining how it works.
- **Per-gemeente chapter pages** + auto-populated event pages; chapters self-fill within a fixed structure.
- **Chapter subscription** emails (low frequency, per region/neighbourhood).
- Mission/Vision pages: **cut internal info, shorten, audience-segment** (engage / already-engaged tools / financing partners). Too much internal org text currently.
- Getting started / "geen fiets" / fietsbibliotheek; KM also **promotes other orgs' services** (radical kindness).
- News feed = articles + downloads (she likes it). Recommendations / "Stad op Kindermaat" toolbox for local governments (advocacy, subsidy-funded); KM may move from lobbying to co-designing solutions.
- Historical archive (city archive / Vlaams leven in Brussel wants KM's flags/posters) — secondary, "because it's tof / proud of it."

## 8. The contested one — private/login organiser area & "who's coming"

- Useful organiser material (checklist, ride archive, manuals, music/rules/videos) is currently **buried in WhatsApp media** and effectively lost. Wants a **per-chapter logged-in area** with that material, an overview, step-by-steps — calm, structured, not "instant info, too much at once." (Mattermost mentioned as a comparison.)
- **"I'm attending" on event pages + showing the hosts/organisers** (not all attendees) as a Facebook-style social nudge; organisers see who's coming. Must be **super simple, few steps**. Possible reward (cap / partner discount for volunteers).
- Why an organiser would make an account: back-office docs, **ride archive** (recycle good past rides — replace the "Volubi Google Doc"; a map of past rides), partner discounts for volunteers.
- **Leticia is ambivalent on whether it's essential:** *"Ik zie het zelf nog niet direct... ik weet niet of dat per se nodig is."* Leans positive for a minimal materials/back-office area; "attending/who's-coming" only if very simple. → still a decision; flagged for the chapter-lead interview.
