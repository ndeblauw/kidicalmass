# Value Proposition

The promise the site makes — overall, then per [persona](20-personas.md). The two headline statements are **working versions locked by Frederik (2026-06-02); only Leticia's sign-off remains** (see [`01-concerns.md`](01-concerns.md) → `S-1`, `S-2`). Per-persona promises follow from them.

## Overall — digital mission statement (working version locked 2026-06-02 — Leticia sign-off pending, `S-1`)

> *"kidicalmass.be is the digital home of a growing Belgian movement — it helps families find their next ride, turns curiosity into participation, lets every chapter run itself, and grows the recurring support that makes the movement financially independent."*

This is the **internal** statement of what the site is for: the four jobs (find · participate · run · fund).

## Hero one-liner — value proposition (working version locked 2026-06-02 — Leticia sign-off pending, `S-2`)

> *"Join thousands of families taking over the streets — a free, festive bike ride for all ages, every month near you."*

Leads on joy + movement scale.

## Per-persona promises

| Persona | The promise |
|---|---|
| **P1 — Family, first-timer** | "In under a minute you'll know if there's a ride near you, when it is, and that your kid can do it." |
| **P2 — Family, regular** | "Your chapter's next date in one place — and one clean page to send a friend, no Facebook required." |
| **P3 — Potential volunteer** | "Pick a role and your chapter; a real local person gets back to you. You'll know what you're signing up for." |
| **P5 — Chapter lead** | "Publish your ride once — it shows on your page and the national calendar. Your team, partners and history, under the national brand." |
| **P6 — Coordination duo** | "Chapters run themselves. You stop being the bottleneck — enough to take two weeks off." |
| **Sponsors/partners** | "Accurate, reliable visibility — your logo where and when it's meant to be." |
