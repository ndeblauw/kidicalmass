## Decisions (current direction)

### Events

- Website is the source of truth; Facebook is distribution (share out, don't host the full detail there).
- Chapters can publish events themselves (remove the central bottleneck).
- People can follow events without Facebook (calendar + email notifications / subscriptions by region).
- Event pages have a public front + a private back for volunteers.
- Route + logistics can be embedded/linked cleanly (e.g. Komoot for routes).

### Chapters

- Chapter autonomy is a core requirement (not a nice-to-have).
- Role-based permissions (no all-or-nothing access).
- Chapters can manage their own partners/sponsors (with guardrails for brand control).

### Volunteers

- Replace the bike@ "email black hole" with structured intake (form) + routing to the right chapter admin.
- Chapter admins can respond to inquiries from inside the platform.
- Volunteer dashboard (upcoming events + who's coming) + attendance tracking.

### Sponsors

- Replace the single PDF/image sponsor block with structured sponsor records (active/inactive, amounts, display rules).
- Sponsor updates must be easy and accurate (process can't lag behind).

---

## Rationale / key insights driving the decisions

- **Manual operations create errors + staleness:** events are maintained one-by-one; the Wix agenda is a hand-edited page.
- **Central bottleneck:** chapters can't self-publish; everything funnels through Leticia.
- **Facebook dependency blocks access:** without Facebook, people can't reliably follow event updates.
- **Onboarding isn't a system:** volunteer routing is email + WhatsApp; follow-up is inconsistent.
- **Delegation is unsafe on Wix:** access control is all-or-nothing.
- **Sponsors/partners aren't data:** a single PDF/image asset can't be maintained or audited.

  **Has she ever estimated how many hours a week go into keeping the site current?**

  **What breaks most often? What's the thing she dreads doing?**

## Facebook dependency — evidence
  **Why does all event detail live on Facebook rather than the website? Deliberate choice or did it just end up that way?**
> *Your answer:*
> - Default + convenience: Facebook is currently the operational event system, while the Wix agenda is manual and high-effort to keep accurate.
> - Facebook is also treated as essential for updates; without a Facebook account it’s hard for people to follow events today.
  **Has she ever tried to put full event info on the Wix site? What happened?**
> *Your answer:*
> - The Wix agenda is a hand-edited page; putting full event details there would multiply the manual work and increase the risk of inconsistencies.
> - Events are already time-consuming and “one-by-one” to maintain, so the team relies on Facebook as the detail layer.
  **If the website could show times, locations and route maps for every parade — would chapters actually use that, or would they still post to Facebook first?**
> *Your answer:*
> - The intent is to reverse the flow: publish the canonical content on the website, then share to Facebook with minimal info so people click through to the original website.
> - This only works if chapters can publish themselves (not via Leticia) and if sharing is frictionless.
  **Does she see Facebook as a long-term dependency or something she'd like to reduce?**
> *Your answer:*
> - She’d like to reduce it by replacing Facebook-following with email notifications / subscriptions by region, and by making the website the primary source of event details.
