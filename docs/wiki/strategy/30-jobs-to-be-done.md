# Jobs-to-be-done

What each [persona](20-personas.md) hires the site to do — the functional core plus the emotional/social weight that makes it land.

| Persona | Functional job | Emotional / social |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 — first-timer** | Find if there's a ride near me soon and whether my kid can do it — fast. | Reassured it's genuinely for us (not a hardcore-cyclist thing); able to tell a friend "we're going." |
| **P2 — regular** | Get the next date + anything special; one clean page to share. | Recruit friends without forcing them through Facebook. |
| **P3 — volunteer** | See how I could help, pick a role + chapter, reach a real local person. | Know what I'm signing up for — not an open-ended commitment. |
| **P5 — chapter lead** *(admin)* | Publish my ride once → chapter page + national calendar, no email to Brussels. | Put my team, partners and history under the national brand. |
| **P6 — duo** *(admin)* | Stop being the bottleneck — chapters self-publish, enquiries route themselves, sponsor info stays accurate. | Trust the system enough to take two weeks off. |
| **Org — money** | Turn a good ride into recurring support — a quiet path from "that was great" → member. | Must never make the family jobs feel transactional. *(Org goal #1; not user-evidenced.)* |
