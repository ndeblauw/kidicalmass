# Organisation Goals

What the organisation needs the site to do for it, **ranked**. User needs lead and org goals follow (see [personas](20-personas.md)) — but when they compete, this is the order.

## Movement facts (context)

Rough current-state numbers (Leticia, 2026-05-18) — sourced reference so they're not reinvented or hardcoded stale:

- ~50 events / season · ~300 active people · ~30 in the national "trekkers" WhatsApp
- ~15–20 local groups (13 listed on the Growfunding campaign)
- ~500-address mailing list (barely used, not localised)
- ~75% of visitors on mobile → mobile-first

## Goals (ranked)

1. **Bring money in.** Grow recurring individual **support** ("spacefunding") so KM can hire and stay self-sufficient. Prefer recurring individual supporters over grant dossiers — *subsidies won't always flow*. → first-class on the site: a dedicated **`/steun-ons`** page, a **"Steun" CTA button in the primary nav** (top-right, replacing the login button), **contextual asks at warm moments** (Home + end of event-detail), **and** the persistent footer CTA. **Framed as *support*, never *membership*** — everyone rides for free; you give so it *stays* free (terminology reworked 2026-06-02, Frederik). *(Top objective, Leticia 2026-05-18; prominence elevated 2026-06-02.)*
2. **Make it effortless for families to find and attend rides.** The primary public job; everything else depends on families showing up.
3. **Convert curious visitors into volunteers** through a clear path from curiosity to contact.
4. **Give the coordination duo their time back.** Less admin, more trust in the system — the "two weeks' holiday" test ([brief](00-strategy-brief.md)).
5. **Grow participation in existing chapters; support new chapters well rather than maximise their number.** *(Reframed from "enable new chapter leads to emerge" — 2026-05-18.)*
6. **Make movement growth legible** (map, chapters, national reach) — mainly for grant applications and new partnerships.
7. **Enable chapter self-publishing within strict design constraints** — no approval flows. *(This is the mechanism behind #4 and #5.)*

## Suspended as formal KPIs (for MVP)

- **North-star metric** — the movement is community-driven, not conversion-driven. "Families who show up to their first ride" is the closest proxy but isn't trackable via the site alone. Suspended for MVP.

## Trade-off note

Goal **#1 (money)** and the family-first emotional register pull against each other on two axes: a support ask must never make the site feel *transactional*, and a *prominent* ask must never read as "you must pay to ride." **Resolution (revised 2026-06-02 — supersedes the earlier "keep it quiet" call):** the ask is now deliberately *prominent* — a "Steun" nav CTA + contextual blocks (Home, end of event-detail) + the footer CTA — because Frederik judged the support goal under-represented. The prominence is bought back with **warmth and a free-riding reassurance on every touchpoint**: the verb is **"steun"** (not "lid"), the moments are warm (just rode / believer), and every ask states that *meefietsen stays free for everyone*. No interstitials. Surface/skeleton lives in [`/steun-ons` brief](../design/30-skeleton/steun-ons.md); pattern in [PAT-10](../design/40-patterns.md).
