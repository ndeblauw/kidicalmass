# Discovery — plan & status

**Method:** understand what's true now about the movement, its users, its current site, and its ecosystem, before deciding strategy.

**Source of truth for Discovery is Notion**, not this repo. The discovery synthesis (desk research, service-design actors & journeys, the deep site audit, user interviews) is maintained in the Notion project *"Kidical Mass Belgium — Website Project"* and is **deliberately not migrated into git** — interview and meeting notes are internal and stay out of the public repo.

This folder exists so the Cascade structure is complete and so later phases can cite Discovery. It is intentionally thin.
