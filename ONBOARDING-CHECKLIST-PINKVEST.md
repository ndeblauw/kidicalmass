# Pink Vest Onboarding Checklist

> For volunteer route marshals (roze hesjes). Your captain will set up your account first.

---

## Step 1: Get Your Account

- [ ] Ask your chapter captain to create your account (or register on the site)
- [ ] Your captain assigns you the **pink vest** role in your chapter
- [ ] You receive a welcome email with login instructions
- [ ] Log in and verify you land on your chapter page

---

## Step 2: Explore the Backstage Hub

- [ ] Go to your chapter's backstage: `/backstage/{chapter-shortname}`
- [ ] Visit each page and get familiar:

### Overzicht (Overview)
- [ ] See the welcome message and recent activity
- [ ] Check the recap of the last ride
- [ ] Check the next ride countdown

### Aan de slag (Getting Started)
- [ ] Read what a pink vest does (4 key things)
- [ ] Watch the embedded video
- [ ] Read the step-by-step first ride guide

### Agenda
- [ ] See upcoming rides in "In voorbereiding"
- [ ] See confirmed rides in "Al vastgelegd"
- [ ] Note the dates and locations of upcoming rides

### Fotos
- [ ] Browse photo galleries from past rides
- [ ] Click on a ride to see its photos

### Groep (Team)
- [ ] See the team roster with all chapter members
- [ ] Check your own listing and role

### Materiaal (Materials)
- [ ] Browse the resources available to pink vests
- [ ] Note the charter, ride guide, and playlist

---

## Step 3: Upload Photos After a Ride

After participating in a ride:

- [ ] Go to **Backstage > Fotos**
- [ ] Find the ride you just completed
- [ ] Click into the photo upload page
- [ ] Upload a **main photo** (the ride's cover image):
  - [ ] Click the upload area
  - [ ] Select a photo from your phone (max 15MB)
  - [ ] Wait for it to upload
- [ ] Upload **gallery photos** (multiple at once):
  - [ ] Click the multi-file upload area
  - [ ] Select multiple photos
  - [ ] Click "Voeg geselecteerde foto's toe"
  - [ ] Wait for all photos to upload
- [ ] Verify your photos appear in the gallery

**Tip:** Photos are processed automatically. If they look blurry at first, wait a minute for the system to generate sharp responsive versions.

---

## Step 4: Stay Engaged

- [ ] Check the **agenda** regularly for upcoming rides
- [ ] After each ride, upload your photos within a few days
- [ ] Check the **overzicht** page for activity updates
- [ ] If you have questions, contact your chapter captain

---

## What You Can and Can't Do

### You CAN:
- View the backstage hub (all pages)
- Upload photos for activities in your chapter
- See the team roster
- See the agenda and materials

### You CAN'T:
- Create or edit activities (captain does this)
- Create or edit articles (captain does this)
- Manage other members' roles (captain does this)
- Access the admin panel (`/admin`)

---

## Need Help?

- **Can't log in?** Ask your captain to verify your account and role
- **Photos not uploading?** Check file size (max 15MB per image, JPG/PNG)
- **Don't see the backstage?** Make sure you're logged in and assigned to a chapter
- **Something else?** Contact your chapter captain
