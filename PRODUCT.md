# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents (and caregivers) who have heard about Kidical Mass and want to find out if it is for them. They are curious but slightly uncertain: is it safe enough? Will their kids enjoy it? Where does it go? The site's job is to answer those questions warmly and quickly, then lower the bar to a first ride. Secondary audience: existing riders returning to check upcoming events and stay connected to the community.

## Product Purpose

Kidical Mass Belgium organises regular group cycling rides for families with children, making cycling streets safer by demonstrating that kids belong on them. The site replaces kidicalmass.be (currently on Wix) and serves as the primary public face of the movement in Belgium: discovery, event listings, chapter info, and community belonging.

## Brand Personality

Playful · Welcoming · Bold

The brand is joyful in the way a neighbourhood cycling day is joyful: real people, kids wobbling on bikes, a sense that anyone can join. It is warm and inclusive without being saccharine. It has a backbone (cycling infrastructure advocacy) but wears it lightly. Boldness shows in colour and visual confidence, not in volume.

Voice register shifts by surface: event pages are warm and concrete; partner/sponsor pages are a notch more serious. See `docs/wiki/tone-of-voice.md` for the full guide.

## Anti-references

- **NGO / charity site** — earnest, stock-photo-heavy, donation CTAs every scroll. This is a community, not a cause.
- **Generic events platform** — Eventbrite/Meetup aesthetic: functional but soulless, no personality.
- **Municipal cycling portal** — government info-architecture, bureaucratic density, grey-on-grey.
- **Loud activist brand** — protest-poster maximalism. Kidical Mass is joyful, not confrontational. Advocacy is present but quiet.

## Design Principles

1. **Joy is the argument.** The site's existence proves cycling with kids is fun, not scary. Every page should feel like evidence of that, not a pitch deck.
2. **Lower the bar, don't raise the ceiling.** The primary visitor is uncertain. Make the first ride feel achievable, not aspirational.
3. **Neighbourhood-first.** This is local and specific, not a global movement brand. Real names, real streets, real kids on bikes beat stock photography every time.
4. **Bold with intention.** The brand has strong colour and playful geometry. Use it decisively, not decoratively. One tilted headline does more than five.
5. **Community over content.** People join because of other people, not because of well-written copy. Faces, rides, and local chapters are the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Site is bilingual (Dutch/French); current build targets NL locale with FR to follow. Reduced-motion opt-out already in `app.css` — keep it maintained. Colour contrast checked against the kidical-ink/yellow/blue palette at AA.
